# Content program: regular social posts (from Nov 27, 2026)

**Founder's direction:** post regularly on topics for employers who are **looking for** or **already have** a 家庭移工 (live-in caregiver or helper). Give real know-how, and promote CareCash as the natural tool, never as a bare ad. Classify the ways employers let workers spend money on groceries and household items, and make that a core theme.

## Audiences
| Stage | Who | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| A. Looking (想請) | Adult children deciding to hire, often first-time under the 80+ no-巴氏量表 rule | Costs, process, what to prepare, how to set house rules and money from day one |
| B. First 3 months (剛請) | New employers | Routines: household cash, food, communication, language, siblings' roles |
| C. Long-term (已請多年) | Experienced employers | Fixing friction: disputes, trust, holidays, replacement caregivers |
| D. The worker (移工) | Indonesian/Vietnamese/Filipino/Thai caregivers | Her rights around money, how records protect her (Indonesian content on /id) |

## Pillars (rotate weekly)
1. **Money methods.** How families give grocery and household money, as a series built on the taxonomy below. The core theme.
2. **Costs and budgeting.** 伙食費, 家用金 amounts, the monthly total cost of a caregiver, siblings splitting costs.
3. **Getting started (stage A).** What to prepare before she arrives; first-week checklist; house rules; language.
4. **Living together.** Communication, food and halal, days off, holidays (Lunar New Year, Eid), respect. Builds trust.
5. **Rules to know.** Work scope, salary versus household cash, no deductions, 1955 hotline. Always general, pointing to the contract, the broker and 1955.
6. **CareCash in practice.** Short how-tos: invite in her language, receipt photos, weekly check. At most 1 in 4 posts.

## Taxonomy: how employers let the worker spend money
| # | Model | In one line | Main risk | CareCash angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 固定週轉金 | Fixed weekly float, top up below a line | Drifts without a weekly check | Balance auto-computed; low-balance alert |
| 2 | 每次給整數、找零歸還 | Round amount per trip; change and receipts left in a fixed spot | Paper pile, no monthly total | Photo instead of paper; monthly total automatic |
| 3 | 先墊後報 | Worker pays first, employer repays | Worker out of pocket; forgotten repayments | Her spending recorded the same day; negative balance shows what's owed |
| 4 | 雇主自己買／網購 | Employer buys; worker does small top-ups only | Small top-ups untracked | Still log the small cash |
| 5 | 固定伙食費自理 | Fixed allowance for her own meals | Mixed up with household cash | Keep it as a separate record from household cash |
| 6 | 店家記帳／儲值卡 | Tab at a local shop or stored-value card | Hard to reconcile | Log top-ups and receipts like cash |

Rule of thumb in every piece: **fixed amount, both sides confirm handovers, keep receipts, check weekly, never deduct from salary.**
