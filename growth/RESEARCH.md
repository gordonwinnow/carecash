# Market research notes (2026-11-25)

Gathered by web search only. Official 勞動部 statistics pages could not be opened from this session, so re-check any number before using it publicly.

## Market
- **家庭看護工:** 215,410 at end of Sept 2026, up ~12.6k in a year (~+1,000/month, record high). Source: 21manpower.com.tw/family-caregiver/41776/
- **Employer households:** ~197k 中重症家庭 employ a caregiver (勞動部, July 2025).
- **Policy driver:** since Aug 2025, people aged 80+ don't need the 巴氏量表. ~200k applications, ~80% under this exemption. This is a wave of **first-time employers**.
- **Nationality:** Indonesia ~78%, Philippines ~13%, Vietnam ~9% (2024, secondary source). Agency estimates for Vietnam run higher (15–18%).
- **長照3.0 (2025-09):** households with a foreign caregiver can now use 日照/家托. A-unit case managers now touch these households, which makes them a channel.

## Competitors / substitutes
- **No direct Taiwan competitor** found for household-cash tracking with two-way confirmation.
- Care Logger (care diary, freemium); HaloCare 家護通 (employer–caregiver translation/chat); KASIH (成大, translation).
- Closest analogue: MaidExpense (Hong Kong). Real substitutes: paper notebook, Excel, LINE notes.
- Broker software (ieI 移工管理系統, 陽光僱傭) covers agency↔employer billing and notices, not household cash. These are complements and possible integration partners.

## Pain evidence
- **Who pays for food, and how much:**
  - An agency claims >70% of families give NT$200–300/day in cash for groceries.
  - Business Today estimates food at ~NT$6k/month.
  - 104職場力 advises 「給適量的錢並記帳」.
- **Thread titles:**
  - PTT elderly 「南部給外勞看護的伙食費…」
  - Dcard 「外籍看護除了薪資和勞健保之外…伙食、醫療費也是雇主負責嗎」
- **1955 hotline (2024):** 202,747 calls; wage complaints 16–20% of complaints. Home caregivers have historically been ~57% of calls.
- **Missing workers:** ~90k migrant workers missing, ~40% caregivers. Not enough food is cited as a reason caregivers leave.

## Rules to respect in messaging
- Salary slips must be bilingual (Chinese + worker's language) and kept 5 years; fine NT$60k–300k. This is a possible premium feature.
- Assigning a caregiver work outside the permitted scope can be fined NT$30k–150k (就服法 §57/§68). Frame spending as household/care spending.
- 就服法 amendment draft (EY approved 2026-04-09): employers and agencies may not take a worker's property, even with consent. Two-way confirmation of cash supports compliance.

## Broker economics
- ~1,200–1,400 licensed cross-border agencies. 2026 評鑑 results are due by 12/31/2026.
- Grades: A ≥90, B 70–89, C <70; absconding rate counts toward the score.
- Lookup: https://agent.wda.gov.tw/agentext/agent/QryAgent.jsp
- Fees: employer ≤NT$2,000 per worker per year; worker NT$1,800/1,700/1,500 per month (years 1/2/3+).
