# 家庭看護工雇主義務：錢與時間點（研究筆記）

Researched 2026-11-27 for the Labs "雇主待辦清單" and "雙語薪資單". Gathered by web search only: www.mol.gov.tw, law.moj.gov.tw, nhi.gov.tw, bli.gov.tw, cdc.gov.tw and laws.gov.taipei could **not** be opened from this session, so most facts below come from search snippets of official pages. Each fact has a confidence level:

- **High**: several official sources agree, or official wording quoted in a snippet.
- **Medium**: one official source, or official but older (rule may have moved).
- **Low**: secondary source only, or sources conflict. Re-check before showing it as fact.

> 以勞動部最新公告為準。This is not legal advice. The app must say so wherever it uses these facts.

Abbreviations: 就服法 = 就業服務法; 雇聘辦法 = 雇主聘僱外國人許可及管理辦法; 勞發署 = 勞動力發展署; 災保法 = 勞工職業災害保險及保護法.

## 1. Monthly money

| Item | Amount | Who pays / how | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salary (薪資)** | **NT$20,000/month minimum** for newly hired and 期滿續聘 家事移工 since 2022-08-10 (was 17,000). MOL suggested +1,000 (21,000) after 3 years with the same employer, +1,000 more (22,000) after 6 years (a recommendation, not a rule). A worker already paid more may not be cut to 20,000. No 2026 change to this figure was found. The general 最低工資 (NT$29,500 from 2026-01-01) does **not** apply: 家事勞工 are outside the 勞動基準法. | Employer, in full, to the worker directly | High for 20,000; Medium for "no change in 2026" |
| **就業安定費** | **NT$2,000/month per 家庭看護工** (家庭幫傭 by a local employer: NT$5,000). **Exempt (免繳)** since 2015-09-01 if the employer or the person cared for is 低收入戶, 中低收入戶, gets 身心障礙者生活補助, or 中低收入老人生活津貼 (the older reduced rates of 600 / 1,200 were replaced by exemption). | Employer. Billed **quarterly**: 勞發署 mails a 繳款單; due by the **25th of Feb, May, Aug and Nov** (for the previous quarter), 30-day grace, then 0.3%/day late fee up to 30%. Pay at convenience stores, post office, bank auto-debit, 全國繳費網 or mobile QR. | High for amounts and exemption; Medium for the 2/5/8/11 due months (from a ~2024 MOL notice) |
| **全民健保 (NHI)** | Worker must be insured; employer is the 投保單位 and must enrol her **within 3 days** of arrival / permit. 投保金額 follows the 基本工資 grade, not the 20,000 salary. 2025 example from 健保署: 28,590 → **NT$1,827/month total**. Split (our calculation, standard 第一類 rule): worker 30% (≈NT$443 in 2025), employer 60% × (1 + avg. dependents 0.56) (≈NT$1,384). **2026 estimate** at 29,500: worker ≈ **NT$458**, employer ≈ NT$1,428, total ≈ 1,886. The employer pays the bill and may deduct the worker's share from salary (show it on the slip). Bills come every 2 months. | Employer pays; worker share deductible | High for "must enrol, 3 days"; Medium for the 2025 figure; **Low for the 2026 split** (our arithmetic: rate 5.17%, 0.56 dependents) |
| **職業災害保險 (職保)** | **Mandatory since 2022-05-01** (災保法 Art. 6) for 家事移工 with a permit. Employer enrols her **on her first day of work**, withdraws on the day she leaves. About **NT$45–48/month**, paid **entirely by the employer**. 勞保局 mails bills every 3 months (Feb/May/Aug/Nov). | Employer only (not deductible) | High |
| **勞保 (一般勞工保險)** | Not required for household employers (the worker is not employed by a 投保單位 under the 勞工保險條例). Only 職保 is mandatory. | — | Medium (sources confirm "職保 mandatory", none states 勞保 explicitly) |
| **膳宿 (food and lodging)** | Employers provide room and board in practice; the 生活照顧服務計畫書 covers 飲食/住宿. Rules on charging 膳宿費 to a 家庭看護工 were **not found**: check the signed contract. Do not deduct anything not in the contract. | — | Low |
| **Rest days / overtime** | 家事移工 are not under the 勞基法, so rest days follow the **contract**. Source-country contract templates (Indonesia, Philippines) require **1 rest day every 7 days**; when she works on it, pay is usually 1 day's wage (月薪 ÷ 30 ≈ NT$667 at 20,000). MOL survey: employers who don't give the day off pay overtime instead; average overtime 2,800/month (June 2025). | Employer | Medium for "contract decides"; Low for the 1/30 formula |
| **特休 / 年終 / 紅包** | No statutory 特休 or 年終 for 家事勞工 (勞基法 does not apply); only if the contract says so. 紅包/年終 are optional. | — | Medium |

## 2. Salary slip (薪資明細表)

- **Rule:** 雇聘辦法 **Art. 66** (current numbering; the old Art. 43 was deleted on 2025-12-30): the employer must give a salary slip printed in **Chinese and the worker's own language**, listing 實領工資, 工資計算項目, 工資總額, 給付方式 and each deduction (健保, 所得稅, 膳宿費 if agreed, court garnishments, other items allowed by law). The worker keeps a copy; the employer **keeps it 5 years**. Confidence: **Medium** (wording seen in a laws.gov.taipei snippet; we could not confirm it is the post-2025-12-30 text).
- Pay in full, directly to the worker (工資全額直接給付). No deductions except those listed above.
- Fine for violations reported as NT$60,000–300,000 (secondary). Confidence: Low.

## 3. Things the employer must not do

- **Keep her passport or ARC** (非法扣留或侵占護照、居留證件或財物): 就服法 Art. 57 §8. High. A 2025 draft amendment adds 6–30萬 fines and bans 保證金; status of that draft not confirmed (Low).
- **Withhold or "save" her salary** (預扣、代為保管薪資) or charge a deposit: forbidden. Medium.
- **Make her do other work** than 家庭看護 (e.g. family shop): 就服法 Art. 57. High.

## 4. One-time and periodic tasks

| Task | When | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **聘前講習** (pre-hire course) | First-time employer of a 家庭看護工/幫傭, **before** the permit is issued (就服法 Art. 48-1). Can be done online. | High |
| **招募許可 → 入國 → 聘僱許可** | For a worker from abroad: employer or agency registers on 移工一站式服務 **5 days before** entry (入國講習, 聘僱許可, 居留). Older rule: apply for the 聘僱許可 within 15 days after entry. | Medium for 5 days; Low for 15 days |
| **外國人生活照顧服務計畫書 / 入國通報** | Submitted with the permit application (two copies, one kept). Change of address: tell the local labour bureau within **7 days**. | Medium |
| **入國健檢 (entry health check)** | Within **3 working days** of arrival, at a designated hospital (受聘僱外國人健康檢查管理辦法 Art. 5). Arrival day = day 0, weekends not counted. | High |
| **定期健檢 (periodic)** | New rules from **2026-01-01** (外國技術人力): within ±30 days of **6, 18 and 30 months after the entry date**. For 家庭看護 after that: chest X-ray + physical **every 3 years** (proof needed when extending or changing employer). Before 2026, the count started from the 工作起始日 for permits from 2016-11-05 on. Missed? Report to the 衛生局 and do it within 7 days. 補充健檢 within 7 days of a new permit if over 1 year since the last check. Fines NT$60k–300k. | High for 6/18/30 + ±30 days; Medium for "from entry date" (new rule) and the 3-year cycle |
| **外僑居留證 (ARC)** | Apply after entry with the residence visa. Immigration Act Art. 22: within **30 days** of the day after entry (移民署 FAQ). An older 移民署 form says 15 days; fine 2,000–10,000 if late. In practice the agency does it through 一站式服務. **Renew before expiry** (usually tied to the permit, max 3 years). | Medium (sources conflict: 30 vs 15 days) |
| **健保 enrol** | Within 3 days (see above). | High |
| **職保 enrol** | On her first working day (see above). | High |
| **意外險 (private accident insurance)** | **No legal requirement found.** Some agencies sell it; 職保 is what the law requires. Optional. | Medium (absence of evidence) |
| **Contract term** | Permit up to **3 years**. At the end: 期滿續聘 (renew with the same worker) or 期滿轉換 (she moves to a new employer). Ask the agency ~4 months before the end (timing not confirmed). Maximum total stay for a 家庭看護工: **12 years**, up to **14** with ≥60 points (就服法 Art. 52); you may apply for the extension once she reaches 11 years. | High for 3 years and 12/14; Low for "4 months before" |
| **離境 / 失聯 通報** | If she is absent without notice for **3 consecutive working days** and cannot be reached, or employment ends, notify the local labour authority, 移民署 and police **in writing within 3 days** (就服法 Art. 56). Not "missing" if she contacted 1955 / was placed in shelter. Fine NT$30k–150k (local gov). | High |
| **健保/職保 withdraw** | On the day she leaves / changes employer. | High |
| **補充保費 (NHI supplementary premium)** | Applies to bonuses over 4× the insured salary in a year, and to "投保單位" payroll gaps. For a household employer with one worker at ~20,000, normally **not relevant**. | Low (not researched in depth) |

## What the app does with this

- **Checklist:** monthly salary + bilingual slip; quarterly 就業安定費 (Feb/May/Aug/Nov 25th, with an "exempt" note); 健保 bill (every 2 months, treated as monthly check); onboarding (entry check in 3 working days, 健保 in 3 days, 職保 on day 1, ARC within 30 days, 生活照顧計畫書); periodic checks at 6/18/30 months after entry; ARC expiry; contract end (3 years from contract start, remind 4 months ahead).
- **Payslip:** Chinese + one chosen language, base 20,000 hint, rest-day pay, 健保 worker share, other deductions with note, net, paid date, two signatures, "keep 5 years" note. It never claims compliance.
