# Broker (外勞仲介) playbook

## Part A: Advisor notes (for the founder)

### What a broker actually cares about, in order
1. **Fewer headaches.**
   - Every employer–caregiver money dispute becomes a LINE call at 10pm, a home visit, a mediation, sometimes a 1955 complaint.
   - Caregivers who feel cheated may transfer (轉換雇主) or abscond (失聯); about 40% of the 90k+ missing migrant workers are caregivers.
   - The absconding rate feeds into the broker's **評鑑 grade** (A/B/C). A C grade can block branches and licence renewal.
2. **Keeping employers happy.** Renewals and referrals are their revenue: employer service fee up to NT$2,000/yr, worker monthly fee NT$1,500–1,800.
3. **Looking modern / caring.** Their blogs and LINE accounts compete on "we take care of you".
4. **Zero work for their staff.** If it adds steps for 業務 or 雙語人員, it dies.

### So the pitch is NOT "a nice app"
The pitch is: **"a free tool you hand to every new family on placement day that cuts grocery-money disputes, and you can see that it's being used."**

### How to approach your contact
- **Goal of meeting 1:** a 30-day pilot with 5–10 of their *new* placements. Not a partnership contract, and no money.
- **Before the meeting** (send me their name, company and role, and I'll prep a one-page brief):
  - Check their 評鑑 grade at https://agent.wda.gov.tw/agentext/agent/QryAgent.jsp.
  - Read their blog/FB to see which topics they already post (grocery money/伙食費 posts = perfect hook).
- **In the meeting (15–20 min):**
  1. Open with a question, not the demo: 「你們一個月大概會接到幾次雇主和看護因為菜錢、家用金起爭執的電話？通常怎麼處理？」 Let them tell the pain.
  2. Demo both sides on two phones: employer gives NT$3,000 → caregiver sees Indonesian, taps 已收到 → takes a receipt photo → employer sees the balance. 2 minutes.
  3. Ask: 「如果新案第一天就請雇主和看護一起裝好，你覺得會有幫助嗎？」
  4. Propose the pilot (below). Ask who on their team does placement-day visits; that person is the real user of our flyer.
- **What NOT to do:** promise features, talk about pricing, or ask for their client list (privacy). We give them a link; *they* share it.
- **After:** a thank-you LINE the same day with the pilot one-pager and their `?ref=` link + QR.
- **Ask for a referral either way:** 「您覺得同業裡誰也會對這個有興趣？」 Also ask whether they sit in a 公會 (trade association). One association talk reaches 50–200 agencies.

### Pilot offer (30 days)
- They pick 5–10 new or recent placements.
- We give: a personal link `carecash.me/?ref=<code>` + QR, a zh-TW employer flyer, an Indonesian/Vietnamese caregiver flyer, and a 5-minute setup we can do by video call for each family if needed.
- We report weekly: how many families started, how many caregivers joined, how many expenses logged. Counts only, no family data.
- At day 30, a 15-minute review:
  - Did disputes/calls go down?
  - Would they roll it out to all new placements?
  - What would make them pay?

### B3. 30-second verbal pitch
> 「雇主跟看護最常吵的，很多都不是大事，是菜錢。錢給了多少、花去哪裡，對不清楚就開始互相懷疑，最後打電話給你們調解。我們做了一個免費工具，雇主給錢、看護按收到，每筆支出拍收據，看護用印尼文操作，雙方看到同一個餘額。我們想找一兩家仲介，在新案第一天就幫雇主和看護裝好，試 30 天看看糾紛電話有沒有變少。」

### B4. Objection handling
| 他們說 | 回答 |
|---|---|
| 雇主不會用 | 只要 Email/LINE 註冊，30 秒建立家庭；我們可以視訊幫每一家設定。試辦期間我們全包。 |
| 看護不會用 / 不想被監視 | 看護用母語介面，最大的好處是保護她：她收到多少錢、花了多少都有雙方確認，不會被冤枉。我們有印尼文說明單張。 |
| 要錢嗎？以後會收費嗎？ | 家庭使用免費。未來若有給仲介的進階功能（例如成效報表），會先跟試辦夥伴討論，試辦夥伴一定有優惠。 |
| 個資安全？ | 每一家的帳只有該家邀請的人看得到。我們給仲介的報告只有數字（幾家在用），不含任何家庭資料。 |
| 我們已經有管理系統（如 ieI、陽光僱傭） | 那些是仲介跟雇主之間的收費與公告；我們管的是雇主跟看護之間的家用金，互補不衝突。 |
| 會不會讓看護多跑腿、違反工作範圍？ | 不會，我們記的是本來就在發生的照顧與家用開銷，不鼓勵額外差事。 |
